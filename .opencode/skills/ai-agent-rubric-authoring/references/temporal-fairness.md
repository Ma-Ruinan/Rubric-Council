# Temporal, version, and source fairness

## Frozen-source tasks

Supplied files are authoritative for facts the task asks to extract or transform. External sources may clarify only when allowed and may not silently override frozen content. Record conflicts and recompute anchors from the frozen artifact.

## Live or recent-window tasks

Use the participant's recorded execution or task cutoff time. Distinguish event date, publication date, update date, forecast horizon, and later commentary. Later facts cannot reduce an earlier answer unless the claim was already unsupported or false at cutoff.

## Versioned software tasks

Evaluate the exact named version and environment. Later code, documentation, vulnerabilities, or defaults may corroborate a diagnosis but are not automatically applicable.

## Unavailable historical evidence

Do not equate present-day link failure with past fabrication. Use stable locators, archives, and contemporaneous corroboration where available. Record limitations. A genuine missing/corrupt benchmark source may block authoring; ordinary verification difficulty does not authorize guessed anchors.
